Hardware protection, warranty, and repair

Hardware warranty

Your Microsoft device or accessory comes with Microsoft's Limited Hardware Warranty. Terms and conditions vary by country or region.

Warranty terms
Get service from Microsoft

To get service for a Surface device, you can send it in to get quality service performed by trusted Microsoft experts using certified parts.

How to get service or repair for Surface Start a service order for a personal device
Authorized service provider support

Microsoft has partners who can provide in-person support for your device as Authorized Service Providers (ASPs). Not available in all countries and regions.

Microsoft Protection Plans

Stay covered, for longer. Protect your device purchase with options for extended hardware and accidental damage coverage. Available in select markets only.

If your device is no longer covered by the standard limited warranty or Microsoft Complete, you may be eligible for out-of-warranty services.
